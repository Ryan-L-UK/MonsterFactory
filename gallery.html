Curated Creatures

Welcome to the curated creatures section. These are hand-picked by Gobby himself. Creatures with unusual quirks, fun stat spreads, or just the right amount of chaos. Each one is generated using the same systems as the main tool, then given a few extra twists, custom artwork, flavour text, and notes on their habits and behaviour. Everything you need to scare, amuse, or thoroughly torment your players with fresh nightmares. They were all approved by Gobby, so if you don't like any of them… blame him.

“Gobby curated these. Gobby has impeccable taste.”

Little Gobby

Psychic • Explosive • Speed-Powered Chaos Monkey

Little Gobby as you've never seen him before: psychic, fast, and far more dangerous than any goblin has the right to be. Turns out the little guy has been hiding a few… surprises.

Spicy Rat

Overcaffeinated • Volatile • Too Hot to Handle

Every adventurer starts with "kill 5 rats", but this one isn't on the menu. The Spicy Rat is volatile, overcaffeinated chaos in fur form, and absolutely too hot to handle. Literally.

Bloodbound Curse-Warden

Ancient • Relentless • Bound by Malediction

Some tombs have traps. This one has a Bloodbound Curse-Warden, a hulking construct stitched together with curses, bad decisions, and enough raw force to make adventurers rethink archaeology.
