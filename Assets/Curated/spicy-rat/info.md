# Spicy Rat

_Overcaffeinated • Volatile • Too Hot to Handle_

---

The Spicy Rat is what happens when a perfectly ordinary sewer rat gets into
a wizard’s spice cabinet and eats something it absolutely wasn’t meant to.
Now it vibrates with unstable heat, leaves tiny scorch marks wherever it
walks, and smells faintly of cinnamon and danger.

Adventurers often underestimate it — right up until it squeaks and the air
around it ignites. The Spicy Rat is fast, twitchy, and aggressively warm to
the touch. Handling one without gloves is strongly discouraged.

---

## Habits & Behaviour

- Constantly emits tiny sparks when excited.
- Runs in frantic zig‑zags, leaving faint burn trails.
- Squeaks loudly when startled, sometimes causing small explosions.
- Attracted to heat sources, campfires, and unattended food.
- Known to set backpacks on fire by accident.

---

## Combat Tendencies

The Spicy Rat is not a measured combatant, it is a volatile skirmisher.
It darts in, heats up the battlefield, and sprints away before anyone can decide whether to chase it or put out the small fires it leaves behind.

- Punishes melee attackers with bursts of accidental heat.
- Uses explosive squeaks to break free when surrounded.
- Relies on speed, panic, and pure spice rather than strategy.
- Prefers to dash into clusters, detonate, and flee before the smoke clears.

## Notes from the Workshop

The Spicy Rat was originally discovered inside a ruined spice merchant’s
basement. The merchant survived, but his stock did not.
