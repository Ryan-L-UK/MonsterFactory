# Little Gobby Goblin

_Psychic • Explosive • Speed‑Powered Chaos Monkey_

---

Little Gobby isn’t just the workshop gremlin who reorganises tools and
accidentally detonates things - he’s also a creature in his own right.
Fast, twitchy, and unpredictably psychic, Gobby moves with the kind of
erratic velocity that suggests he’s powered by caffeine, spite, and
arcane static electricity.

He’s small, loud, and constantly vibrating with energy he absolutely
should not have access to. When startled (which is often), he releases
bursts of psychic noise strong enough to rattle armour plates and
confuse spellcasters. When excited (which is always), he sprints in
circles at speeds that defy physics and common sense.

---

## Habits & Behaviour

- Treats “danger” as a suggestion rather than a rule.
- Moves so fast he occasionally forgets to take the floor with him.
- Emits psychic squeaks that may or may not count as a language.
- Collects unstable objects because “they hum nicely.”
- Has a 50/50 chance of helping or causing an explosion. Sometimes both.

---

## Combat Tendencies

Gobby is not a frontline fighter, he is a **mobile hazard**.  
He darts in, causes chaos, and vanishes before anyone can decide whose
problem he is.

- Uses bursts of psychic energy when cornered.
- Relies on speed over strength, confusion over strategy.
- Prefers to “assist” allies by distracting enemies with noise, motion,
  and occasionally throwing something that was definitely not meant to be thrown.

---

## Notes from the Workshop

Gobby insists he is “perfectly safe.”  
This is demonstrably untrue.

Still, he’s become an essential part of the workshop ecosystem - a
one‑goblin storm of enthusiasm, psychic static, and questionable ideas.
